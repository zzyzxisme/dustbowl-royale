🧟 Wasteland tips

  • Straw Dogs hunt in packs near the farm.
  • Carrion Crows dive-bomb from above — watch their shadows.
  • Glow Rats swarm out of junk piles.
  • The Bloat Hog guards Radio Hill and drops rare loot.
  • KO'd players drop a stash — loot it with E.
  • Red barrels go boom. Green goo hurts.
